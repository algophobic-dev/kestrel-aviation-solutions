About Kestrel Aviation Solutions

We build aviation software that makes flying simpler and more affordable.

What we do

Our first product is Kestrel EFB, a free electronic flight bag. It puts charts, weather, routes, approach plates, a preflight briefing and a logbook in one app for Mac, Windows, iPad and iPhone.

Kestrel Aviation Solutions was started in 2026 by Vincent Ye, a student pilot and developer in Livingston, New Jersey. It began with a simple question: why does good flight planning software cost hundreds of dollars a year? Kestrel EFB was the answer: a full-featured flight bag that anyone can use for free. Today we’re a small team building more tools for pilots, students, instructors and the wider aviation community, and we build them around what our users ask for.

Who it's for

Pilots, student pilots, flight instructors and anyone else in aviation.

What it costs

Kestrel EFB is free, with no account needed to use it.

Where we are

Livingston, New Jersey
